TapThat Light Help & setup guide

Without a sidecar, TapThat is Light: comment, Export, paste into your agent. Connect a sidecar and pages on your dev sites get a Send to Claude button that sends comments straight to an agent running beside the dev server.

Sidecar

Your developer gives you both values (what you need). Both are printed when the sidecar starts. Behind a proxy (Railway and similar), the URL ends in /__tapthat.

One per line. Leave empty to use the sidecar's own list. Every other site stays Light.

Claude credential

The agent runs on your own account. Paste an API key (sk-ant-…) or a token from claude setup-token. It is sent once to your sidecar, stored there encrypted, and never kept in the browser. It is checked the first time you apply. Skip this if your sidecar has its own key. How do I get one?
