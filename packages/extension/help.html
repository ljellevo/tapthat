TapThat help

Leave comments directly on a website, the way you'd comment on a design, and let an AI assistant make the change for you. This page explains how to set it up and what you need.

What TapThat does

You click on anything on a web page, such as a heading, a button or an image, and write what you'd like changed. TapThat can then do one of two things with your comments:

Copy them

Press Export and all your comments are copied, ready to paste to a developer or into an AI tool.

Works on any website. Needs no setup at all.

Apply them

Press Apply to dev and an AI assistant makes the change on your team's test version of the site. You see it update in front of you, usually within a minute.

Only on your team's test site, and needs a little setup.

If you only want to copy comments, you're done: the extension already works. The rest of this page is about Apply to dev.

What you need

From your developer (they set this up once for the team)

  • The address of the test site, for example https://dev.yourcompany.com. This is where you'll leave comments.
  • The "Sidecar URL". The address TapThat sends your comments to. It often looks like the test site's address with /__tapthat on the end.
  • An access token. A long password that lets TapThat talk to your team's test site. Keep it to yourself, like any password.

From you (only if your developer asks for it)

  • A Claude key. The AI assistant that makes the changes is Claude, made by Anthropic. Its work is paid for by whoever's key is used. Some teams share one key and you won't need your own; otherwise you'll use your own. How to get one ↓
Not sure whether you need your own key? Skip it. TapThat will ask for it the first time you press Apply to dev, and only if it's actually needed.

Getting a Claude key

A Claude key is a long code that starts with sk-ant-. It lets TapThat use Claude on your account.

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign in, or create an account.
  2. Under Billing, add a payment method or buy some credit. The key won't work on an account with no credit.
  3. Open API Keys and click Create Key. Name it something like TapThat.
  4. Copy the key right away. It's shown only once. If you lose it, delete it and create a new one.
  5. Paste it into TapThat (see step 4 below). Don't save it anywhere else, and don't send it in chat or email.
Your developer may give you a different kind of key, one that starts with sk-ant-oat01-. That works too; paste it in exactly the same place.

Setting it up

This takes about two minutes. You only do it once.

  1. Open TapThat's settings. Right-click the TapThat icon in your browser's toolbar and choose Options. If you don't see the icon, click the puzzle-piece icon first.
  2. Paste the Sidecar URL and the access token from your developer into the first two boxes. Leave "Sites that get Apply to dev" empty: it fills itself in.
  3. Click "Save & test". You should see a green ✓ and the word Connected. If you see a red ✗ instead, check that you copied both values exactly, or see Something's wrong.
  4. Add your Claude key (if you need one): paste it in the Claude credential box and click Save credential. Afterwards you'll only ever see its last four characters, like •••• 4f2a.

That's it. Open the test site and you'll find a round TapThat button in the corner.

Using it

  1. On the test site, click the round TapThat button in the corner, or press Alt + Shift + C.
  2. Hover over the page. A blue outline shows what you'd be commenting on. The ↑ and ↓ keys pick a bigger or smaller area.
  3. Click it and write what should change, as you'd say it to a colleague: "Make this button green", "This heading should say Welcome back". Save with ⌘ + Enter (Mac) or Ctrl + Enter.
  4. Add as many comments as you like, then press Apply to dev.
  5. Watch the page. It updates by itself, and each comment shows how far along it is:
QueuedWaiting its turn. Someone else's changes are being made first.
EditingClaude is working on it. The panel shows what it's doing.
LiveThe change is on the page. Have a look!
CommittedDone, and saved so your developers can review it.
Build brokenThe change was made but broke something. Nothing was saved. Add a comment describing the problem, or let your developer know.
FailedNothing was changed. The panel says why in plain text.
UndoneYou pressed Undo and the change was taken back.

When it's done

  • Like it? Press Resolve to tick off those comments.
  • Don't like it? Press Undo to take the change back, or write another comment to adjust it.
Changes are made on the test site only. The real website your customers use doesn't change until your developers review and publish them.

Safety & cost

Where is my Claude key kept?
It's sent once to your team's test server and stored there encrypted. It is never saved in your browser, and it's never shown again. You'll only see its last four characters.
Can I take it back?
Yes. In TapThat's settings, click Disconnect and the test server deletes it. You can also delete the key itself on console.anthropic.com at any time.
What does it cost?
Each time you press Apply to dev, Claude's work is billed to the account whose key was used. A few small changes are usually inexpensive. You can see your usage and set a spending limit on console.anthropic.com. TapThat also caps how many times per hour a key can be used, in case something goes wrong.
Which websites can it change?
Only your team's test site, the one your developer connected. On every other website, TapThat can only copy comments. It never sends anything anywhere.

Something's wrong

I don't see an "Apply to dev" button
You're either not on the test site, or TapThat isn't connected yet. Check the address you're on, then open TapThat's settings and click Test connection.
"Can't reach the sidecar"
The test server is off or restarting. Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, tell your developer. You can still press Export to copy your comments.
"The sidecar rejected the token"
The access token doesn't match. Ask your developer for the current one and paste it again.
"Your saved key no longer works"
Paste your Claude key again. This happens when the test server was reset. If it still fails, check on console.anthropic.com that the key exists and your account has credit.
It says "Queued" for a long time
Changes are made one at a time, so someone else is probably ahead of you. The panel shows how many.
"The page changed since you commented"
Someone else's change landed after you wrote your comments. Look at the page again before pressing Apply, in case what you wanted is already different.
Anything else
Every error message in the panel has a Copy button. Paste it to your developer: it tells them exactly what happened.
